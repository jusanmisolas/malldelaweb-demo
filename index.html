Practice Areas

Agrarian Law

We assist with agricultural land disputes, tenancy issues, agrarian reform matters, and related administrative proceedings.

Contract Drafting and Review

We draft, review, and revise contracts for individuals and businesses to clarify terms and protect their interests.

Corporate Formation and Registration

We help with incorporation, company registration, regulatory requirements, and corporate documents.

Corporate Secretarial Services

We prepare board resolutions, meeting minutes, corporate records, regulatory filings, and other governance documents.

Correction of Birth Records and Company Information

We help correct errors in civil registry records and company information through the appropriate procedures.

Estate Planning

We assist with wills, estate settlement, succession, and the transfer of assets to heirs and beneficiaries.

Family Law

We assist with marriage, annulment, separation, child custody, support, adoption, and other family matters.

Litigation

We represent clients in administrative cases, disciplinary proceedings, regulatory disputes, and matters before government agencies.

Notarial Services

We notarize affidavits, contracts, deeds, special powers of attorney, and other documents, subject to legal requirements.

Retained Legal Officer
