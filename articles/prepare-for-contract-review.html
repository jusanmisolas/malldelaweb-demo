Contract Drafting and Review

What to gather before a contract review

Bring the latest complete draft, including schedules, exhibits, and earlier written terms. The Civil Code treats a valid contract as binding between the parties. A review should therefore start with what the parties are actually promising and who is authorized to make those promises.

Check the essentials

Article 1318 of the Civil Code identifies consent, a definite object, and cause as essential requisites. The need for a particular form depends on the transaction. Ask counsel to flag any signature, authority, or form issue before execution.

What to gather

Check the practical details against the draft. Identify the price, payment dates, deliverables, acceptance criteria, and the person responsible for each step. Ask counsel to review provisions on termination, liability, delays, and dispute resolution alongside the main terms.

What to discuss

Flag any deadline to sign and any work or payment already made. Mark clauses you cannot change or do not understand. A clear list helps counsel distinguish business decisions from legal concerns and identify any formalities that apply to the transaction.
