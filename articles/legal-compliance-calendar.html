How to build a legal compliance calendar

What to prepare

Start with dates your team can verify from contracts, permits, and official notices. Record the source of each deadline and who owns the task. Give recurring items a lead time for gathering records and securing approvals.

What to check next

Review the calendar with counsel when the business changes activities, opens a location, or takes on a new agreement. Keep evidence of completed filings or notices with the corresponding calendar entry.
