Estate Planning

What to prepare for an estate planning meeting

List the property, obligations, and people you want to discuss. Include real estate, business interests, and jointly held assets. The Civil Code’s succession rules can affect what may pass under a will and which heirs have a protected share.

Discuss the form of the will

The Civil Code sets different formalities for ordinary and holographic wills. Under Article 810, a holographic will must be entirely written, dated, and signed by the testator’s own hand. Ask counsel which form fits your situation.

What to gather

Bring available titles, agreements, and any existing will. Describe how property is held and flag questions about marriage, prior relationships, or family members who depend on you. These facts help counsel assess the estate and the people affected.

What to discuss

Discuss practical goals such as who should manage assets, which property you hope to preserve, and how to cover obligations. Ask about the formal requirements for any document before signing it.
