Notarial Services

How to prepare for an in-person notarial visit

Tell the office what document you need notarized, who will sign it, and where it will be submitted. Mention any special instructions from the receiving office and whether anyone has already signed.

Bring a complete document

The 2004 Rules on Notarial Practice address incomplete documents and competent evidence of identity. Ask the office which original identification and supporting documents to bring, and check any instructions from the recipient.

What to gather

The rules for in-person notarization generally require the signer to appear before the notary and establish identity as prescribed by the rules. Ask which identification and supporting documents the office can accept. If you are signing for someone else or for a company, ask what proof of authority to bring.

What to discuss

Review names, dates, and attachments before your appointment. Bring the complete document and confirm the number of copies needed. Ask the office when to sign, since the required steps depend on the notarial act.

Philippine legal sources

  • Supreme Court compilation, 2004 Rules on Notarial Practice
  • Amendment to the rules on competent evidence of identity

This guide provides general information. The facts and applicable rules determine the next steps in a particular matter.
